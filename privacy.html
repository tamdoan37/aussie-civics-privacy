Privacy Policy — Aussie Civics Last updated: November 22, 2026 Aussie Civics ("the app") is a free study aid for the Australian citizenship test. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains — in plain language — what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no trackers, and no analytics. Everything you do stays on your device. What is stored, and where The app saves the following only on your device (using the operating system's local storage). It is never uploaded to us or anyone else: Your first name (from the setup screen), and your test date if you choose to enter it Your study progress, quiz history (including your values scores), an unfinished quiz, your flashcard position, and the answers you've marked as preferred Your settings (voice, daily goal, reminder preferences) Because this data lives only on your device, we cannot see it, and it is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data (Settings → Reset app data, which also cancels any scheduled reminders). Permissions Notifications (optional): The app asks only when you turn on the daily study reminder. The reminders are scheduled on your device and are not sent through us or any server. Audio: Listen Mode and the read-aloud buttons play voice clips in Australian accents that are built into the app. Where no clip exists (and for "Hear the voice" in Settings), the app uses your device's built-in speech engine. No audio is recorded. If your device is set to use an online voice, your device's speech service (not the app) may process the text it reads. The app does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, location, or photos. Network use The app works fully offline and does not download anything. Links to the Department of Home Affairs, the Our Common Bond booklet, its licence, or the app store open in your browser or store app only when you tap them, and send none of your data. Children's privacy The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Since no data is collected at all, it is safe for all ages. Third parties We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or any third-party service that would receive your information. We do not sell or share any data, because none is collected. Not affiliated with the government Aussie Civics is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Department of Home Affairs or the Australian Government. Its questions are original practice questions, not the questions used in the real test. Always check the current rules at immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/citizenship/test-and-interview.
